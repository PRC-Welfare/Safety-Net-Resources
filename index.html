Have Community Cats? Get Them Fixed for Free!

As a Spartanburg County resident, this is a 100% FREE resource for you.

✓ Fully financed by Spartanburg County — 100% Free for county residents! Bring photo ID showing your Spartanburg County address.
Official Guidelines

Animal Allies TNR Program

📍 820 Gossett Road, Spartanburg, SC 29307

📞 Call (864) 576-6971
Drop-Off Days

Tues, Wed & Thursdays

Drop-off: 7:30 AM – 8:00 AM

Daily Capacity

First 20 cats in line

Cut-off is 8:15 AM (or when full)

Pick-Up Time

4:00 PM Same Day

Clinic closes promptly at 4:30 PM

Trap & Handling Rules:

• One cat per trap: Cat MUST be in a humane feral cat trap, NOT a regular carrier.
• Need a trap? Traps can be borrowed at CatTrapDepot.org.
✓ What’s Included: Spay/neuter surgery, core rabies and distemper vaccines, and a humane left ear-tip.
• Left Ear-Tip: A clean 3/8" flat clip on the left ear is the universal sign the outdoor cat has been fixed. Please do not re-trap!
